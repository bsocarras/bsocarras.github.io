Privacy Policy

Brief is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. Brief collects anonymized data about the app’s performance and user’s device. Anonymized data refers to information that cannot reasonably be used to identify an individual, either directly or indirectly. The collected anonymized data is used to analyze Brief’s performance. The collected anonymized data is not shared with or sold to any third party.

Brief utilizes Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, which automatically collects certain information about your device and how you interact with Brief. Google acts as a data subprocessor on our behalf. This data includes technical information such as your device type, operating system, and app version. Firebase Analytics also collects information about your usage of the app, including the screens you view, the features you use, and the duration of your sessions. This helps us understand user behavior, identify trends, and improve Brief's performance and features. We may also collect certain events automatically, such as when you first open the Brief or when the app crashes. On iOS devices, Firebase may collect your Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), depending on your device’s privacy settings and your consent. You can manage these settings in your iOS privacy controls. This information is used in an aggregated and anonymous form to provide insights into app usage and is not used to personally identify you.

We use reasonable measures to help protect the information we possess. You should understand that no data storage system or data transmitted over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You provide us with this data at your own risk. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, we do not accept liability for unintentional disclosure.

Brief is designed for and targeted to audiences residing in the United States and governed by United States law. If you are located outside of the U.S., by sending us your data you consent to its transfer to and storage within the United States.

Protecting the privacy of young children is important to us. Brief is not directed to children under 13 (or other age as required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you learn that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, you may contact us. If we learn that we have collected any personal information in violation of applicable law, we will promptly take steps to delete such information.

We may access or disclose information when required by law or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect user safety, or enforce our rights. If we receive a subpoena, warrant, discovery order, or other request or order from a law enforcement agency, court, other governmental entity, or litigant that seeks your data, we will make reasonable attempts to direct the requesting party to seek the data directly from you. Nothing in this policy is intended to limit any legal defenses or objections that you have to a third party’s (including a government’s) request to disclose your information.

By using Brief, you agree to be legally bound by and comply with this policy. If you do not agree to comply with this policy, you are not authorized to use Brief.

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):
